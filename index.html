Updated October 19, 2026

Summary of a report compiled Oct 8, 2026 (with an Oct 7–9 recheck). Trump post times are Mountain Time (MT). Q-drop dates are US Eastern dates as shown on qalerts.app.

Bottom line

Verified Q-drop echoes by @realDonaldTrump, Aug 1 – Oct 8, 2026: 0, out of 1,655 unique archived status pages checked (1,794 entries).

No post's text, image description, or video transcript contained "Nothing can stop what is coming"/NCSWIC, "Where we go one, we go all"/WWG1WGA, "The storm is coming," "Calm before the storm," "Trust the plan," "Q+," "Great Awakening," "Patriots are in control," or a Q symbol. No news report (Media Matters, NBC, CNN, AP, Rolling Stone, Guardian, Independent) of such a post in this window was found. An Oct 7–9 recheck of 29 unique posts also found no matches.

Method

  1. Trump posts: every trumpstruth.org entry dated Aug 1 – Oct 8, 2026 (last capture Oct 8, 4:57 PM MT), regex-searched across full page text, image descriptions and video transcripts. The 11 bare-link re-posts were checked via the public Truth Social status API.
  2. News and social: web searches of major outlets and Snopes, plus an X search for claims that "Trump posted a Q phrase."
  3. Drops: the full qanon.pub dataset (4,966 drops; qalerts.app copy identical), numbered chronologically and spot-checked on qalerts.app (#38, #55, #513, #668, #791, #2494, #4951). Eras: 4chan /pol/ Oct 28 – ~Dec 1, 2017 (#1 to ~#240); 8chan Nov 25, 2017 – Jul 31/Aug 1, 2019 (to #3570); 8kun from Nov 2, 2019 (#3571 on).
